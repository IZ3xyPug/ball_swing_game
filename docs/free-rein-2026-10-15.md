## 2. The sky and the planet

### 2.1 Overlays now sit exactly on the planet (alignment fix)
- Found: the renderer fills an object's box by scaling its picture to
  **cover** it (cropping the overflow). The sky's box is ~3% wider than the
  sky picture, so the picture overflows it top and bottom by ~37 px. Layers
  that map sky-picture rows with a plain stretch were off by up to ~25 px —
  at night the lit band at Earth's horizon stayed lit because Earth's night
  layer started below it.
- New helper `background::sky_picture_rect(c)` returns where the picture
  really lands. Earth's night layer, the ocean's moving water, and the
  weather over the planet (lightning, storm eye) all map through it now.
- Checked: full-resolution crops of the horizon by day and night — the night
  now darkens from exactly the baked horizon.
- (The nebula and the aurora still use the plain mapping on purpose: they
  were placed by eye against it and are not pinned to anything baked in.)

### 2.2 Earth's night matches Earth's day
- Earth's day was rebuilt as an ocean planet under the photo's clouds, but
  its night layer was still made from the old fog-coloured photo. The night
  is now made from Earth's actual daytime sky picture (`earth_day_sky`),
  shaded down, so day and night are the same planet.
- City lights no longer shine through thick cloud: they fade out where the
  day picture is cloud-white (and no dark specks are left where they faded).
- Checked: day/night crops of the horizon.

### 2.3 Faster sky build
- Building the sky pictures (the starfield sky in each background colour,
  with Earth's ocean-and-cloud surface) took ~1.6 s on a background thread at
  start-up; a very quick first PLAY would have waited for it. The five
  background colours are now built side by side, and Earth's surface rows are
  split across the cores: ~0.8 s. Output is unchanged.
- `START_PROFILE=1` now also prints how long this took (`BG_IMAGES built in`).

### 2.4 The shop shows the new Earth
- The SKY shop's EARTH card and preview were cut from the bare photo (all
  cloud, the old fog look). They now come from Earth's actual sky picture, so
  the card shows the blue planet you get.

## 4. Leaderboard: each mode ranked by what it's about

Before, all three columns ranked by distance — but `GameMode::record_kind`
already said NORMAL is about score and BOSS RUSH about time.

- **CASUAL** — by distance (unchanged).
- **NORMAL** — by score (distance breaks ties).
- **BOSS RUSH** — by bosses beaten, then the fastest time for them
  (shown as `4 • 6:32`).
- Each card's heading now says what it ranks by ("BY SCORE" …).
- The board file keeps score, bosses and time now
  (`mode|distance|day|score|bosses|secs|name`); the first version's lines
  still load. Old NORMAL/BOSS RUSH rows with no score/bosses drop out (they
  cannot be ranked fairly), and the first-run seeding only seeds CASUAL.
- The death screen's summary now says when a run makes the board:
  `NEW BEST!  •  #2`, or `#4 ON THE LEADERBOARD`.

### 4.1 Bug fixed: bosses beaten were under-counted
- The run's boss count (`boss_index`) only advances when you leave the
  victory pause after a kill. A run that ended before that — die in the
  victory stasis, or a boss rush cut short — counted one boss too few, on the
  death screen's BOSSES figure as well. A new `bosses_beaten` counter is
  bumped at the kill itself (`finish_boss`) and is what the summary and the
  leaderboard use. (`boss_index` still drives the schedule, unchanged.)
- Checked: a headless boss rush (`HEADLESS_MODE=2`, forced win, then death)
  records `bosses 1, 0:24` and places #1; before, `bosses 0` and no place.

### 4.2 New test switches
- `HEADLESS_MODE=<0|1|2>` sets the run's mode; `RUN_RECORD_LOG=1` prints what
  a run's end records.
- Tests: `each_mode_ranks_by_its_own_measure`, `the_old_file_format_still_reads`
  (199 → 201).

## 5. Small fixes and polish

### 5.1 Shop: W/S no longer touch the removed rope colour
- In the ROPES carousel, W/S (and up/down) still stepped the rope *colour* —
  a setting that was removed (ropes keep their own colour). Each press
  silently rebuilt the menu cat's rope and wrote the profile to disk for
  nothing. Those keys now do nothing there.

### 5.2 Death screen: the "NEW BEST" line moved
- It sat right on top of the distance figure, crowding it (worse now that it
  can also say the leaderboard place). It sits on the card's header row, to
  the left of RUN SUMMARY. Checked in a capture.

### 5.3 Checked, no change needed
- Game tick: ~0.5–0.7 ms a frame in a normal run (QUARTZ_TICK_PROFILE).
- Pause stats' coin count and the HUD's are the same counter.
- Daily challenge texts match their effects; GLASS CAT really does turn off
  SECOND WIND (`free_respawns_left = 0`).
- The gravity-flip HUD indicator and timer are already hidden.

### 5.4 Ocean water finds its planet by id
- The moving sea (and its pre-build) recognised the OCEAN WORLD as "planet
  number 3". Reordering the planet list would have silently put the water on
  the wrong world. It now looks the planet up by its id (`"ocean"`).

### 5.5 Achievement text: Aurora Chaser
- Auroras now only come over the ICE WORLD, so "Swing under the northern
  lights for a whole display" gave no hint how. It now reads "Over the Ice
  World, swing under the northern lights for a whole display." (Still fits on
  one line on the achievements page — checked.)

### 5.6 Shop card labels stay on one line
- Long labels ("DESERT WORLD • 400 META") wrapped onto a second line under the
  card. Labels over 21 characters now shrink (down to 19 px from 28) to fit.

### 5.7 Bigger planet pictures on the SKY cards
- Planet cards showed a thin strip of horizon in a lot of empty card. Cards
  now use a closer, taller crop of the middle of the horizon
  (`sky::planet_card_art`); the preview under the carousel keeps the wide
  strip. Checked in a capture.

### 5.8 Settings page: spacing and colours
- "EFFECTS" sat flush against the graphics buttons above it. The rows are
  re-spaced evenly (sliders at 720/930/1140, GRAPHICS at 1340, EFFECTS at
  1630); everything still sits inside its card.
- Slider tracks were the old bright blue (main-menu settings) and grey (pause
  settings); both are the midnight lilac now.
- Slider click/drag hit-testing reads the same row constants, so it follows.
  Checked in a capture.

### 5.9 Profile rows say something useful
- Each profile row ended in "New" unless the tutorial was finished — so a
  profile many runs in could still read "New". It now shows "New" only for a
  profile that has never played ("Ready" if it did the tutorial but never
  ran); otherwise `12 runs • Best 21,644 px`. Checked in a capture (fits the
  row).

## 6. Debug switches added in this pass (all off unless set)
| Switch | What it does |
|---|---|
| `START_PROFILE=1` | times the game's start-up steps, and the sky-picture build |
| `ATLAS_LOG=<px>` | names every texture upload at least that big (renderer) |
| `SKY_CLIMB_LOG=1` | prints the sky / nebula / far planet's screen height |
| `RUN_RECORD_LOG=1` | prints what a run's end records (mode, score, bosses, place) |
| `HEADLESS_MODE=<0-2>` | headless run in Casual / Normal / Boss Rush |
| `HEADLESS_EVENTS=a@t,b@t` | fire any number of custom events at ticks |
| `HEADLESS_PAUSE_AT=<f>` | open the pause menu at a frame |
| `HEADLESS_SKY=…,planet=n,night,events` | sky options for a headless run |
